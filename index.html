PhotoSpectra

Your photos, on your own PhotoPrism server.

An iOS app for who self-host PhotoPrism. It uploads your camera roll without duplicates and lets you browse the server from your phone. No accounts, no tracking, nothing in between.

Read the docs

iPhone · iOS 15.1 or later

What's in the app

Uploads each photo once

Photos are hashed on the phone and checked against the server first. Whatever is already there gets skipped, so syncing the whole camera roll is safe.

Your server, in your pocket

The Remote tab shows what's on PhotoPrism: albums, videos, live photos. Open anything full screen.

All the metadata

Camera, lens, aperture, exposure, ISO, location and time zone, as PhotoPrism stores them.

Backs up overnight

Runs once a day in the background. Wi-Fi only if you want.

How syncing works

  1. On your iPhone

    PhotoSpectra reads your library and computes a SHA1 hash for each photo. Nothing leaves the phone yet.

  2. Check

    Each hash is compared with what your server already has. Matches are marked as synced and skipped.

  3. On your server

    The rest go up over WebDAV, into your PhotoPrism server

Before you start

  • A PhotoPrism server: on a NAS, a Raspberry Pi, a VPS, wherever you run it.
  • An iPhone with iOS 15.1 or later.
